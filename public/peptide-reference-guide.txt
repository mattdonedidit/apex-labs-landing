================================================================================

Welcome to the APEX LABS Research Peptide Reference Guide.

This document provides comprehensive, evidence-based information on 10 
widely-studied peptide compounds used in biomedical and cellular research. 
Each profile includes structural background, documented research applications, 
dosage ranges for research contexts, stability information, and storage 
guidelines.

individuals with formal training in biochemistry, cell biology, or related fields.

PROFILE 1: BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound)
---

FULL NAME: Body Protection Compound-157
CLASSIFICATION: Synthetic pentadecapeptide

STRUCTURAL ORIGIN & BACKGROUND
BPC-157 is a synthetic peptide derived from body protection compounds found in 
gastric juice. It consists of 15 amino acids and has been extensively studied 
in both in vitro and animal models for its protective and regenerative 
properties.

DOCUMENTED RESEARCH AREAS
• Tissue repair and regeneration (muscle, tendon, ligament)
• Angiogenesis (blood vessel formation)
• Neuroprotection and neuroplasticity
• Gastrointestinal mucosa protection

RESEARCH DOSAGE RANGES (in vitro/in vivo)
In Vitro:        10–1000 nM concentrations
Animal Studies:  1–10 μg/kg per injection

PHARMACOKINETICS
Half-life:  ~6–8 hours (in vivo)
Duration:   Effects observed 24–72h post-administration

STORAGE & STABILITY
Store at 2–8°C (refrigerated). Protect from light. Reconstituted solutions 
stable for 7–14 days when kept refrigerated. For long-term storage, freeze at 
–20°C or below.

---
PROFILE 2: TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4 Analogue)
---

FULL NAME: Thymosin Beta-4
CLASSIFICATION: 43-amino acid peptide

STRUCTURAL ORIGIN & BACKGROUND
TB-500 is a naturally occurring peptide found in thymus tissue and serum. 
Research versions are synthetic analogues of thymosin beta-4, known for immune 
modulation and tissue repair properties. It regulates actin dynamics and 
cellular migration.

DOCUMENTED RESEARCH AREAS
• Cell migration and tissue remodeling
• Angiogenesis (neovascularization)
• Collagen deposition and wound healing
• Myogenic differentiation

RESEARCH DOSAGE RANGES (in vitro/in vivo)
In Vitro:        1–100 nM concentrations
Animal Studies:  2–20 mg/kg per injection

PHARMACOKINETICS
Half-life:  ~2 hours (in vivo)
Duration:   Biological effects persist 48–96h despite short half-life

STORAGE & STABILITY
Store at 2–8°C in sealed vials. Avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles. 
Reconstituted solutions stable for 10–14 days refrigerated. Lyophilized 
(powder) form stable for 24+ months at 2–8°C.

---
PROFILE 3: Tirzepatide (GIP/GLP-1 Receptor Agonist)
---

FULL NAME: Tirzepatide (GIP/GLP-1 Receptor Agonist)
CLASSIFICATION: 39-amino acid peptide analog

STRUCTURAL ORIGIN & BACKGROUND
Tirzepatide is a dual glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) and 
glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist. It activates both GIP and 
GLP-1 signaling pathways simultaneously, making it a unique compound in 
metabolic research.

DOCUMENTED RESEARCH AREAS
• Glucose homeostasis and insulin secretion
• Metabolic regulation and weight dynamics
• Incretin system function
• Cellular signaling and energy metabolism

RESEARCH DOSAGE RANGES (in vitro/in vivo)
In Vitro:        0.1–100 nM concentrations
Animal Studies:  0.1–3 mg/kg per injection

PHARMACOKINETICS
Half-life:  ~5 days (in vivo, extended release)
Duration:   Steady-state effects at 4–5 week intervals

STORAGE & STABILITY
Store at 2–8°C in original container. Protect from light and freezing. Once 
reconstituted, stable for 28 days refrigerated. Pre-filled pens should not be 
frozen.

---
PROFILE 4: Retatrutide (GLP-1/GIP/Glucagon Receptor Agonist)
---

FULL NAME: Retatrutide (Triple Receptor Agonist)
CLASSIFICATION: Synthetic peptide, 34 amino acids

STRUCTURAL ORIGIN & BACKGROUND
Retatrutide is a novel triple receptor agonist that activates GLP-1, GIP, and 
glucagon receptors simultaneously. This tri-agonist approach represents 
advanced metabolic research into multi-pathway regulation.

DOCUMENTED RESEARCH AREAS
• Metabolic regulation across multiple pathways
• Glucose homeostasis and gluconeogenesis
• Lipid metabolism and mitochondrial function
• Multi-tissue endocrine signaling

RESEARCH DOSAGE RANGES (in vitro/in vivo)
In Vitro:        0.01–10 nM concentrations
Animal Studies:  0.05–2 mg/kg per injection

PHARMACOKINETICS
Half-life:  ~6–7 days (in vivo)
Duration:   Cumulative effects observed over 2–4 weeks

STORAGE & STABILITY
Store at 2–8°C. Protect from freezing and light exposure. Reconstituted 
solutions remain stable for 14–21 days under refrigeration. Do not reuse once 
opened.

PROFILE 5: NAD+ (Nicotinamide Dinucleotide)
---

FULL NAME: β-Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide
CLASSIFICATION: Cofactor, nucleotide

STRUCTURAL ORIGIN & BACKGROUND
NAD+ is an essential coenzyme found in all living cells. It plays a central 
role in metabolic redox reactions and serves as a substrate for NAD+-consuming 
enzymes including sirtuins and PARPs. Research-grade NAD+ is synthetically 
produced and used in various cellular studies.

DOCUMENTED RESEARCH AREAS
• Mitochondrial function and ATP production
• Sirtuins activation and cellular aging models
• DNA repair pathways (PARP signaling)
• NAD+ metabolism and metabolic adaptation

RESEARCH DOSAGE RANGES (in vitro/in vivo)
In Vitro:        100 μM–10 mM concentrations
Animal Studies:  250–1000 mg/kg per injection

PHARMACOKINETICS
Half-life:  ~30–60 minutes (in vivo)
Duration:   Acute effects; chronic studies use repeated doses

STORAGE & STABILITY
Store at 2–8°C away from light. NAD+ is sensitive to hydrolysis; use fresh 
solutions when possible. Unopened vials stable for 12–24 months. Reconstituted 
solutions should be used within 24 hours.

---
PROFILE 6: PT-141 (Melanotan II Analogue)
---

FULL NAME: Bremelanotide (PT-141)
CLASSIFICATION: 7-amino acid peptide, melanocortin agonist

STRUCTURAL ORIGIN & BACKGROUND
PT-141 (Bremelanotide) is a melanocortin-1A receptor agonist derived from 
alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone (α-MSH). It was developed as a research 
compound for studying neuroendocrine signaling and melanocortin pathways.

DOCUMENTED RESEARCH AREAS
• Melanocortin receptor signaling
• Neuroendocrine function and hormone regulation
• Pigmentation and melanocyte biology
• Central nervous system receptor pharmacology

RESEARCH DOSAGE RANGES (in vitro/in vivo)
In Vitro:        1–100 nM concentrations
Animal Studies:  0.1–1 mg/kg per injection

PHARMACOKINETICS
Half-life:  ~2–3 hours (in vivo)
Duration:   Peak effects at 30–60 minutes post-injection

STORAGE & STABILITY
Store at 2–8°C in sealed vials. Protect from light and moisture. Reconstituted 
solutions stable for 7–10 days refrigerated. Lyophilized form stable for 18+ 
months.

---
PROFILE 7: Sermorelin (GHRH Analogue)
---

FULL NAME: Sermorelin Acetate (GHRH 1–29 analogue)
CLASSIFICATION: 29-amino acid peptide

STRUCTURAL ORIGIN & BACKGROUND
Sermorelin is a synthetic analogue of growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH). 
It consists of the first 29 amino acids of the natural 44-amino acid GHRH and 
is used in research examining growth hormone secretion and somatotropic axis 
signaling.

DOCUMENTED RESEARCH AREAS
• Growth hormone-releasing hormone receptor signaling
• Somatotropic axis function
• Anterior pituitary hormone secretion
• Aging models and neuroendocrine regulation

RESEARCH DOSAGE RANGES (in vitro/in vivo)
In Vitro:        0.1–100 nM concentrations
Animal Studies:  1–10 μg/kg per injection

PHARMACOKINETICS
Half-life:  ~8–10 minutes (in vivo)
Duration:   GH response peaks at 30–45 minutes post-injection

STORAGE & STABILITY
Store at 2–8°C. Protect from freezing (unless lyophilized). Reconstituted 
solutions stable for 7–14 days refrigerated. Avoid exposure to heat and direct 
light.

---
PROFILE 8: Ipamorelin (GH Secretagogue)
---

FULL NAME: Ipamorelin (Growth Hormone Secretagogue)
CLASSIFICATION: Pentapeptide

STRUCTURAL ORIGIN & BACKGROUND
Ipamorelin is a pentapeptide growth hormone secretagogue (GHS) that selectively 
stimulates GH release through ghrelin receptor signaling. Unlike other GHS 
compounds, ipamorelin shows minimal effects on cortisol and prolactin, making 
it valuable for selective GH axis research.

DOCUMENTED RESEARCH AREAS
• Ghrelin receptor (GHSR-1a) agonism
• Growth hormone secretion and regulation
• Selective GH axis stimulation without hormone dysregulation
• Metabolic research and muscle protein synthesis pathways

RESEARCH DOSAGE RANGES (in vitro/in vivo)
In Vitro:        0.1–100 nM concentrations
Animal Studies:  0.5–5 μg/kg per injection

PHARMACOKINETICS
Half-life:  ~2–3 hours (in vivo)
Duration:   GH peak at 30–60 minutes; effects persist 2–4 hours

STORAGE & STABILITY
Store at 2–8°C. Protect from freezing (unless lyophilized). Reconstituted 
solutions stable for 7–10 days refrigerated. Avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles.

---
PROFILE 9: HGH (Human Growth Hormone)
---

FULL NAME: Somatotropin (191-amino acid peptide)
CLASSIFICATION: Recombinant polypeptide hormone

STRUCTURAL ORIGIN & BACKGROUND
HGH is a 191-amino acid peptide hormone produced by the anterior pituitary 
gland. Research-grade HGH is manufactured using recombinant DNA technology. It 
plays crucial roles in growth, metabolism, and cellular turnover, making it 
extensively studied in endocrinology and aging research.

DOCUMENTED RESEARCH AREAS
• Growth hormone receptor (GHR) signaling
• Cellular proliferation and differentiation
• Metabolic regulation and lipid metabolism
• Protein synthesis and cellular aging models

RESEARCH DOSAGE RANGES (in vitro/in vivo)
In Vitro:        1–1000 ng/mL concentrations
Animal Studies:  1–10 μg/kg per injection

PHARMACOKINETICS
Half-life:  ~15–20 minutes (in vivo)
Duration:   Hormonal effects persist 6–12 hours; IGF-1 effects longer-term

STORAGE & STABILITY
Store at 2–8°C. Protect from freezing of liquid form (unless specified 
lyophilized). Reconstituted solutions stable for 14–28 days refrigerated. Avoid 
shaking; roll gently to mix. Unopened vials stable for 24+ months.

---
PROFILE 10: IGF-1 LR3 (Insulin-Like Growth Factor-1 Long R3)
---

FULL NAME: Insulin-Like Growth Factor-1 Long R3 (70-amino acid peptide)
CLASSIFICATION: Recombinant growth factor analogue

STRUCTURAL ORIGIN & BACKGROUND
IGF-1 LR3 is a modified analogue of insulin-like growth factor-1 (IGF-1). It 
features an extended N-terminus and increased resistance to IGF-binding 
proteins, resulting in prolonged biological half-life compared to native IGF-1. 
Used extensively in cellular and tissue culture research.

DOCUMENTED RESEARCH AREAS
• IGF-1 receptor (IGF-1R) signaling and proliferation
• Myogenic differentiation and muscle growth
• Neuronal survival and neuroprotection
• Cellular metabolism and protein synthesis

RESEARCH DOSAGE RANGES (in vitro/in vivo)
In Vitro:        1–100 ng/mL concentrations
Animal Studies:  20–100 μg/kg per injection

PHARMACOKINETICS
Half-life:  ~12–24 hours (in vivo, extended due to LR3 modification)
Duration:   Sustained effects over 24–48 hours per injection

STORAGE & STABILITY
Store at 2–8°C. Protect from freezing and light. Reconstituted solutions 
stable for 7–14 days refrigerated. Extended half-life analogue may require 
slightly more careful handling than native IGF-1. Avoid vigorous agitation.
